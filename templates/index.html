Our Story

How It Began

✿

Thanks to an introduction from a family friend, we met for the first time at And Some Flower Cafe. We were both a little hesitant about going on a blind date, but it didn't take long for us to realize how much we had in common. Conversation flowed easily, and one date quickly turned into many. Before the month was over, we officially became a couple.

Ever since, life has been a series of adventures. We've travelled to amazing places, introduced each other to new hobbies, and even supported each other through a few surgeries along the way. Through every adventure and challenge, we've realized that as long as we have each other, everything always finds its way.

Now we're gearing up for our biggest, brightest adventure yet, and we can't wait to celebrate it with you when we say "I do."

The Day

Ceremony & Reception

✿
3:30 PM

The Ceremony

Join us at The Ridge Okotoks, where the rolling Alberta foothills stretch out beneath an open sky. Expect fresh air, soft grass underfoot, and plenty of afternoon sunshine as we exchange our vows surrounded by the people we love most. Seating will be provided, and the ceremony will last about 30 minutes. To make sure no one misses a moment, we'd love for you to arrive by 3:00 PM.

5:30 PM

The Reception

As the evening settles in, we'll move into a beautiful canopy tent for a plated dinner, an open bar, and a few signature cocktails crafted just for the night. Expect warm light, heartfelt toasts, and laughter spilling out into the foothills as we celebrate together.

Important Details

What to Know

✿

Dress Code

We're going semi-formal for the big day, so feel free to pull out your favourite outfit. Think cocktail dresses, midi or maxi looks, dressy jumpsuits, sharp suits, or a crisp shirt with slacks and a blazer. For the ladies, the ceremony will be entirely on grass, so so we recommend shoes with a thicker heel to keep you comfortable. Bonus points if your outfit is something you can party in, because we plan on doing plenty of that with you!

Location & Parking

The ceremony and reception will be held at The Ridge Okotoks, located at 300, 344153 5th Street West. On-site parking is available; please follow the road to the rear of the venue, where staff will be on hand to direct you. Taxis and ride-shares can drop off and pick up directly at the venue.
