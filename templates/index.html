Our Story

How It Began

✿

Our story began in 2022, when a mutual family friend introduced us and we went on a blind date at a coffee shop. What started as a simple first meeting quickly turned into something special, as we instantly connected and found ourselves laughing and hitting it off.

Before long, one date turned into many, and within a month we officially began dating. Since then, we've shared so many memories, laughter, adventures, and milestones together — and we can't wait to share the next one with you.
