A Note From Us

Getting Ready Together

✿

Here’s the plan for hair and makeup on the morning of the wedding. We’ve staggered the times so everyone has space to relax, sip coffee, have a bite to eat, and enjoy the morning without feeling rushed. Please arrive in our matching bridal PJs so we can take cute getting ready photos! Some requests from the makeup and hair artists include:

  • Please ensure you are prepared to avoid leaving the chair whilst the artist is working.
  • Please ensure teeth are cleaned, you have been to the toilet and contact lenses are applied (if applicable), before sitting in the styling chair.
  • Please do not eat or speak on your mobile phone during makeup application and hairstyling.
  • Please inform the bride in advance of any allergies or reactions regarding makeup application or hair styling.
  • Please do not have any makeup applied and free from products except for daily skin care routine. The artist will prep your skin for you.
  • Please ensure hair is clean, completely dry, free from any kind of product and do not use any straighteners or curling tongs on your newly washed hair. You are advised to wash hair the night before and avoid any heavy conditioners. If you have wet hair, you will be expected to dry it thoroughly before the service begins.

Looking forward to getting ready with everyone!

✨ Rainki
